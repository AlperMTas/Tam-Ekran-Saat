### ✨ Öne Çıkan Özellikler

*   **OLED Dostu Animasyonlu Masa Saati:** Şık "Flip Clock" animasyonları ve saf siyah (#000000) arka plan. Ekran yanması (Burn-in) koruması içerir.
*   **Akıllı Odak Modu (Pomodoro Timer):** "Rahatsız Etmeyin" (DND) modu ile tam entegre. Arka planda güvenilir bir şekilde çalışır ve süre bittiğinde sistem bildirimi (ve konfeti animasyonu) gönderir.
*   **Gelişmiş Takvim ve Görev Yönetimi:** Aylık ve Haftalık görünümler, kategorize edilmiş renkli notlar ve tekrar eden görev desteği.
*   **Biyoritim ve Üretkenlik Analizi:** Hangi saatlerde daha verimli olduğunuzu gösteren Akıllı Isı Haritası (Heatmap).
*   **Ana Ekran Widget'ı:** Yeniden boyutlandırılabilir görev widget'ı ile planlarınız her an göz önünde.

## 🇬🇧 English

Transform your phone or tablet into a stylish digital desk clock, a smart focus tool, and a comprehensive planner. Stay in the moment without distractions and boost your productivity!

### 📥 Download Now (APK)
You can download the latest compiled APK file to install the app directly on your phone:
👉 **[Download the Latest Release (APK)](https://github.com/AlperMTas/Tam-Ekran-Saat/releases/latest)**

### 🎯 Purpose of the App
"Tam Ekran Saat" (Full Screen Clock) is designed to prevent your phone from being a distraction while you work or study. With its minimalist design, it's easy on the eyes and organizes your life with advanced focus tools and a smart agenda.

### ✨ Key Features

*   **OLED-Friendly Animated Desk Clock:** Elegant "Flip Clock" animations with a pure black (#000000) background. Includes built-in Burn-in protection.
*   **Smart Focus Mode (Pomodoro Timer):** Fully integrated with "Do Not Disturb" (DND) mode. Runs reliably in the background and sends a high-priority system notification (with a confetti animation!) when the timer finishes.
*   **Advanced Calendar & Task Management:** Monthly and Weekly views, categorized color-coded notes, and recurring task support.
*   **Biorhythm & Productivity Analysis:** A smart Heatmap that shows you which hours you are most productive.
*   **Home Screen Widget:** A resizable task widget to keep your daily plans always in sight.

### 🚀 Installation (How to Run)
This is a fully local Android application built using **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose**. 
You do not need any API keys or server setup.

1. Download or clone this repository (`git clone`).
2. Open the project using **Android Studio**.
3. Wait for the Gradle synchronization to finish.
4. Click the "Run" button to test the app on your virtual or physical device.
